TECHNOVA UNIVERSITY — UNIVERSITY OVERVIEW

TechNova University is a modern educational institution focused on providing quality education, practical learning, research opportunities, and professional development.

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, computer science, business administration, data science, information technology, and related disciplines.

The university promotes practical learning through laboratory sessions, workshops, seminars, technical competitions, internships, and industry interaction programs.

Students have access to academic departments, laboratories, a central library, computer facilities, student support services, sports facilities, and campus activity centers.

The university encourages students to participate in research projects, innovation activities, technical clubs, cultural events, and community development programs.

The academic year is divided into semesters. Each semester includes classroom instruction, practical sessions, assignments, internal assessments, and final examinations.

The university provides both academic and extracurricular opportunities to help students develop technical knowledge, communication skills, teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving abilities.

Students are expected to follow university policies, maintain academic discipline, respect faculty and staff, and use university facilities responsibly.
