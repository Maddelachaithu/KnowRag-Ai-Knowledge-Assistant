TECHNOVA UNIVERSITY — STUDENT SERVICES

TechNova University provides several services to support students during their academic journey.

The Student Support Center helps students with general academic guidance, administrative information, and university-related queries.

The Career Development Center provides career guidance, resume preparation sessions, interview preparation, internship information, placement training, and industry interaction opportunities.

The university provides counseling and student welfare services to support students with academic and personal development needs.

Students can participate in technical clubs, cultural clubs, sports activities, innovation programs, entrepreneurship activities, and community service programs.

The university organizes workshops and seminars to help students improve communication skills, leadership abilities, technical knowledge, teamwork, and professional skills.

Students can contact the appropriate university department when they require information about academic records, examinations, certificates, internships, or other administrative services.

Students are encouraged to actively participate in university activities while maintaining their academic responsibilities.
