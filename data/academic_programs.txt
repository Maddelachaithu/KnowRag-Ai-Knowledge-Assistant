TECHNOVA UNIVERSITY — ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

TechNova University offers a wide variety of academic programs, degree courses, and curricula for students.

Undergraduate and Postgraduate Academic Programs Offered by the University:
- Academic Programs in Engineering & Technology: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science, Information Technology, and Data Science.
- Academic Programs in Management & Business: Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) and Master of Business Administration (MBA).
- Postgraduate & Advanced Degree Programs: Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Computer Science.
- Doctoral Academic Research Programs: Ph.D. in Engineering and Management disciplines.

Degree Course Curriculum and Structure:
All academic degree programs offered by the university combine theoretical courses, laboratory sessions, projects, assignments, and examinations. Academic departments regularly organize workshops, guest seminars, and student projects.
