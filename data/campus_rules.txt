TECHNOVA UNIVERSITY — CAMPUS RULES

All students are expected to maintain discipline and behave respectfully on the university campus.

Students must carry their valid university identification card while on campus and present it when requested by authorized university personnel.

Students must attend classes, practical sessions, examinations, and other academic activities according to the schedules provided by the university.

Students must respect faculty members, staff, visitors, and fellow students.

University classrooms, laboratories, libraries, sports facilities, and other resources must be used responsibly.

Students must not intentionally damage university property.

Students must keep the campus clean and use designated waste disposal facilities.

Electronic devices should be used responsibly during academic activities and must not disturb classes or examinations.

Students must follow safety instructions when using laboratories, workshops, sports facilities, and other specialized areas.

Students are expected to follow examination rules and academic integrity requirements.

Any student activity that violates university policies may be reviewed according to the applicable disciplinary procedures.

Students should contact university authorities when they need clarification about campus rules or policies.
