TECHNOVA UNIVERSITY — LIBRARY SERVICES

The TechNova University Central Library provides academic resources and learning facilities for students, faculty, and researchers.

The library contains textbooks, reference books, journals, magazines, newspapers, research publications, project reports, and digital learning resources.

Students can use the library to read books, access academic resources, study individually, and conduct research.

The library provides computer terminals for accessing digital academic resources and online databases.

Students must carry their valid university identification card when using library services.

Books borrowed from the library must be returned before the specified due date. Students are responsible for keeping borrowed materials in good condition.

Library users must maintain silence and avoid activities that disturb other students.

Food and beverages are not permitted in the main reading areas.

Students should follow library instructions and return books to the appropriate location after use.

The library may provide assistance with finding academic resources, research materials, journals, and reference information.
